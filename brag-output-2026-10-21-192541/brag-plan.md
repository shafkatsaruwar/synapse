# Brag Plan: Synapse Health Companion

## What is this app?
Synapse is a personal health companion that brings medications, check-ins, recovery tools, and daily health context together on one Today homepage.

## The angle
Scattered healthcare tools float as independent glass panels, then magnetically assemble into the real Synapse Today homepage. Not a feature montage — one deterministic assembly story.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
Five translucent Synapse UI panels float apart in soft white studio space: greeting, Medications, How I’m Feeling, Sick Mode, bottom navigation. They read like separate apps.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Panels glide into their exact Today-screen positions and lock as one homepage.
- Assembled interface rotates into a crisp front-facing product reveal.
- Brand close: Synapse mark + “Your health life, finally in one place.”

## Outro / punchline
Clean white field. Synapse logo. Tagline. “Available on the App Store.”

## User flow worth showing
Open Today → see medications / feeling / Sick Mode together → understand one companion holds the day.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (mapped from Apple-style product commercial)
- Creative direction: soft white studio, translucent glass, burgundy accents, magnetic assembly
- Interpretation: restrained pacing, elegant typography, no chaotic cuts; motion does the storytelling

## Format: vertical — 1080×1920
## Duration: 15 seconds
## Frame rate: 30 fps

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Fragmented — 3s
Soft white studio. Greeting, Medications, How I’m Feeling, Sick Mode, and bottom nav float as separate glass panels with depth and soft shadows. Subtle independent drift.
Sequential/interaction: panels present as independent tools
Audio intent: quiet, slightly unfinished
Audio-coupled idea: soft ambient presence; no hard hits
Music: low bed
Transition mood: soft magnetic pull → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Unified — 5s
Camera eases back. Each panel moves on a deterministic path into its exact Today homepage slot (greeting → meds → Your health row with Appointments + How I’m Feeling → Sick Mode → bottom nav). Soft shadows settle; glass edges align.
Sequential/interaction: yes — magnetic assembly into homepage
Audio intent: resolution / order arriving
Audio-coupled idea: soft drop/switch as each major panel locks
Music: bed continues; optional cue near assembly mid-point
Transition mood: clean hold → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Product reveal — 4s
Assembled homepage (inside a minimal phone frame) rotates to front-facing. Crisp, readable Synapse UI. Hold.
Sequential/interaction: none beyond camera settle
Audio intent: product confidence
Audio-coupled idea: none
Music: bed; strong-cue optional near 8.7s
Transition mood: soft dissolve → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Brand reveal — 3s
Clean white. Synapse logo. “Your health life, finally in one place.” Support: “Available on the App Store.”
Sequential/interaction: logo + lines settle
Audio intent: quiet close
Audio-coupled idea: soft logo land
Music: fade under final hold
