# Hyperframes Composition Brief: Synapse

## Objective
Update both existing Synapse trailers so every app screen is a supplied real screenshot.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output-2026-10-14-010220/composition/`
- Portrait: `brag-output-2026-10-14-010220/composition-portrait/`
- Renders: `brag.mp4` (1920×1080) and `brag-vertical.mp4` (1080×1920)
- Duration: 23 seconds, 30 fps

## Source Material
- Real iPhone captures (1206×2622) copied as named assets
- Official icon: `composition/assets/icon.png`
- Do not redraw UI

## Creative Direction
- Tone: cinematic + app-store
- Camera, lighting, and type move. Screenshot pixels do not.
- Device frame fits the capture aspect. No extra notch or status bar.
- 16:9: headline left, large device right, then readable detail crops
- 9:16: headline above, phone ~60–70% of canvas height

## Copy that must appear verbatim
- Your health. Your story.
- Meet Synapse.
- Every dose. Organized.
- Every visit. In one place.
- Check in with your recovery.
- Your health information. Your control.
- Local storage by default. Optional connected features.
- Synapse.
- Your health, connected.

## Avoid
- Fabricated screens
- Simulated taps, slider moves, checkmarks, or App Lock ON
- Baking a dashboard poster as frame 0
- Epic / MyChart, HIPAA, never-leaves-the-device

## Audio
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12, volume ~0.16
- SFX: two soft hits only
- Audio-reactive: subtle glow only
