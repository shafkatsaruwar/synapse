# Synapse — Full App Overview

A plain-language guide to what Synapse is, what it contains, and how it helps real people manage everyday health.

**Product name:** Synapse Health Companion  
**iOS:** [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/synapse-health-companion/id6759972036)  
**Android:** In progress  
**Active codebase:** `synapse-reset`

> Synapse helps you organize care. It does **not** diagnose, prescribe, or replace your doctor, pharmacist, or emergency services.

---

## What Synapse is

Synapse is a **personal health companion** on your phone. It keeps the pieces of your care life in one place:

- Medications and reminders  
- Appointments and visit prep  
- How you’re feeling day to day  
- Health records you want handy  
- Recovery tools when you’re sick  
- An emergency card when seconds matter  

It is built **local-first**: your health details live on your device first. Optional sign-in, iCloud/CloudKit backup, Apple Health, and caregiver sharing are available when you want them — they are not required to use the core app.

You can use Synapse in **Full Mode** (everything organized in hubs) or **Simple Mode** (fewer steps, focused on meds, appointments, and feeling).

---

## Who it’s for

Real people who:

- Take one or many medications and want fewer missed doses  
- Juggle doctor visits and want prep notes in one spot  
- Live with chronic illness, adrenal / stress-dose protocols, or recovery days  
- Care for someone else and need a clearer picture of their day  
- Want a quieter Mental Health Day or fasting-aware Ramadan support  
- Prefer privacy-friendly tools that stay useful offline  

You do not need to be “techy.” Onboarding asks your name and one useful starting point — add a med, add an appointment, log how you feel, or just explore.

## What the app contains

### Home: Today

Your daily landing page. See what’s due, what’s coming up, and shortcuts into Sick Mode / Health Status, medications, and other priorities without hunting through menus.

### Medications

- Scheduled meds and as-needed (PRN) meds  
- Dose logging (“took it” / “not yet”)  
- Reminders and repeat schedules  
- Pharmacy, doctor, refill / supply tracking  
- Sick-day / stress-dose instructions when you’ve set them  
- Scan a label to help add a med  

### Appointments

- Upcoming visits on a calendar  
- Doctor linking, notes, and prep  
- Reschedule, cancel, mark done  
- Import from Apple Calendar or scan an appointment card  
- Travel / leave-by hints when location is available (iOS)  

### How I’m Feeling

- **Daily Log** — mood, energy, sleep, quick check-in  
- **Symptoms** — name an episode, severity, notes; fever can offer Sick Mode  
- **Monthly Check-in** — a fuller monthly snapshot  

### Health Records

- **Vitals** — blood pressure, weight, and similar  
- **Lab Work** — results and notes  
- **Imaging** — scans and imaging notes  
- **Timeline** — health history over time  
- **Reports** — visit-ready summaries from what you’ve logged  
- Optional documents / insights when those features are enabled  

### Wellbeing

- **Eating & Hydration** — meals and water  
- **Mental Health Day** — quieter tools for harder days  
- **Mood Lifters** — small comfort ideas (also surface in Sick Mode)  
- **Goals** — gentle goals you set yourself  
- **Cycle Tracking** — optional  
- **Ramadan Mode** — fasting-aware daily support  

### Care & Support

- **Doctors** and **Pharmacies** contact lists  
- **Caregiver Dashboard** and **Managed Person** (when you’re in a caregiver role)  

### Emergency

- **Emergency Card** — critical info (allergies, contacts, key meds) you can keep ready, export, or save to your phone  

### You & Settings

- Personal profile, conditions, allergy / emergency info  
- Vaccines and surgeries  
- Preferences (appearance, Simple vs Full Mode, navigation)  
- Care & Sharing (roles, caregiver connections)  
- Integrations (Apple Health, assistant access)  
- Privacy (app lock, local-first posture)  
- Backup & Restore  
- Help & Feedback, About Synapse  

### Modes that reshape the day

- **Sick Mode** — recovery protocol: temperature check-ins, hydration/food/rest, stress dosing, focused navigation  
- **Simple Mode** — shorter add flows and a tighter tab bar  
- **Ramadan Mode** — fasting-aware timing and logs  

### Home-screen widgets (iOS)

Sixteen WidgetKit tiles — next med, hydration quick-sip, PRN log, pain, sleep, sick mode, recovery, flare forecast, appointment prep, caregiver, mental health, labs, overview, daily log, medication day, 14-day report. Tap opens the right place in the app; some can log a sip or PRN dose from the home screen.

## What it can do for real people

### “I take pills every day and forget”

Add your medications with times. Synapse reminds you, lets you mark doses taken, shows today’s progress, and can put **Next Medication** or **Medication Day** on your iPhone home screen.

### “I have a doctor visit next week and always forget what to ask”

Log the appointment, attach notes, keep doctor info nearby, and use **Reports** built from what you’ve already logged so you’re not starting from a blank page in the waiting room.

### “I’m sick and my steroid dose changes”

Turn on **Sick Mode**. Follow check-ins, log temperature, track hydration and food, and see stress-dose instructions for the meds you’ve configured. When fever eases, Recovery Mode helps you wind down until you’re ready to turn it off.

### “I care for my parent”

Use a **caregiver** role, managed-person details, and the caregiver dashboard / widget to see what needs attention — missed meds, next steps, quick log actions — without rebuilding their whole life in a spreadsheet.

### “Some days my brain is loud”

Open **Mental Health Day** for a quieter toolkit, or use Mood Lifters and a simple daily log instead of a clinic-style form.

### “It’s Ramadan and my med timing changes”

Use **Ramadan Mode** so fasting-aware times and daily logs match how your days actually run.

### “Something went wrong and EMTs need facts fast”

Keep an **Emergency Card** with allergies, contacts, and critical context you can export or save to your phone before you need it.

### “I want help without giving up my privacy”

Use Synapse offline by default. Turn on backup, Apple Health, sign-in, or assistant access only when you choose — see Privacy and Integrations in **You & Settings**.

## How the app is organized day to day

**Full Mode bottom tabs:** Today · Health · Quick Add · Emergency · You  

**Simple Mode bottom tabs:** Today · Medications · Appointments · How I’m Feeling · You  

**Health hub** (Full Mode) opens into Medications, Appointments, Feeling, Records, Wellbeing, and Care & Support.

**Quick Add** is a radial shortcut to common actions (for example add a med or appointment) without digging through menus.
